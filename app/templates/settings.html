Rules for “{{ campaign.name }}”

Morality scale
Scores run from minus this number to plus this number, with 0 as neutral.
Lawful / Chaotic threshold
A law score at or above +this is Lawful; at or below −this is Chaotic; anything between is Neutral.
Good / Evil threshold
Same rule on the good–evil axis.
Temporary HP stacking
By the rules, new temp HP replaces old temp HP only if it's higher. Turn on to add them together instead (homebrew).
Everything rolls to hit
Every attack and harmful spell rolls a d20 plus its bonus against the target's AC; no bonus means +0. Healing and helpful spells just land. Turn off to use saving throws for spells that have them (book rules).
Spell slots
Off (house rule): no spell slots. Spells are used like attacks, as often as their cooldown allows. On: levelled spells spend a slot from the sheet.
Concentration saves
When a caster who's concentrating takes damage, roll their Constitution save (DC 10 or half the damage) automatically. A failed save ends the spell on everyone. Off: the log just reminds you to call for it.
Death saves: ally bonus
A hero at 0 HP adds this to death saving throws while a hero who's still standing is right next to them on the battle map. 0 turns it off.
Initiative ties
How combatants with the same initiative roll are ordered.
Auto-sort turn order
Re-sort whenever an initiative is entered or rolled. Turn off to arrange the order yourself.
Skip these on “Next turn”
Unconscious characters are not skipped by default because they still roll death saves.

What players can change

For friends who joined from their own device (see Players). They only ever change their own character.

Their HP
Take damage, heal, add temp HP, fix their HP and mark death saves. In a fight, it shows in the combat log and you can undo it.
Spell slots
Spend and get back their own spell slots.
Casting on the party
Cast their healing and buff spells (Cure Wounds, Bless, Shield of Faith…) on themselves or anyone in the party. Healing is rolled for them.
Hit dice
Spend hit dice to heal on a short rest (rolls the die + Constitution).

How to restore a database snapshot
  1. Stop the app (close its window, or press Ctrl+C in it).
  2. Open the data folder inside the app folder. Rename dnd_tracker.sqlite3 to something like dnd_tracker.broken.sqlite3 so you still have it.
  3. Delete dnd_tracker.sqlite3-wal and dnd_tracker.sqlite3-shm if they exist.
  4. Copy the snapshot you want from data\backups into data and rename it to dnd_tracker.sqlite3.
  5. Start the app again.
