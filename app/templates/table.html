How this works, and other free ways
  • Go online starts a free Cloudflare “quick tunnel”: a private https link to this computer. No account, no router settings, no cost. The link changes each time you start it, and stops when you click Stop or close the app.
  • Anyone with the link reaches the join page only. They still need your join code, and people coming in through the link are never treated as the DM.
  • Keep this computer on and the app running while you play. Everything stays saved here.
  • Tailscale (free) is the private alternative: friends install Tailscale, you share this computer with them, and they open http://<your Tailscale IP>:5050/join. Start the app with start-lan.bat for that.
  • Full guide: ONLINE.md in the app folder.

Playing with friends who aren't on your Wi-Fi
  1. Easiest and private: Tailscale. You and each friend install the free Tailscale app and sign in to the same Tailscale network (you invite them). Start Campaign Ledger with --lan, then friends open http://<your Tailscale IP>:5050/join. Nothing is exposed to the public internet.
  2. A public link: a tunnel such as Cloudflare Tunnel (cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:5050) gives you a temporary https address to send. Anyone with the link can reach the join page, but they still need your join code, and visitors through a tunnel are never treated as the DM.
